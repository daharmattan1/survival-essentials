## Synthesis

Survival is triage under stress. The **Rule of Threes** (≈3 minutes without air, ≈3 hours without
shelter in harsh conditions, ≈3 days without water, ≈3 weeks without food) sets the order of
operations so effort goes to what kills you soonest — most people invert this and chase food while
dehydration or exposure is the real clock. **STOP** (Stop, Think, Observe, Plan) is the circuit
breaker that stops panic from spending the calories and good decisions you cannot replace. And the
**psychology / will to live** is not soft material: under stress, the people who survive are the
ones who keep making small correct decisions instead of freezing. These three govern how every
downstream domain is prioritized.
